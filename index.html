OUR SERVICES
Reliable, Certified Tech for Business at Scale
End-to-End Device Refurbishment
We restore used tech devices to like new condition using over 179 quality checkpoints, component level repairs, and system optimization. Perfect for enterprise scale deployment.
Global Tech Sourcing and Distribution
Leverage our international supply chain to source high demand refurbished laptops, phones, and tablets with fast and certified delivery through strategic logistics and warehousing.
Enterprise Ready Refurbished Inventory
Choose from a wide range of tested and certified laptops, smartphones, tablets, and accessories. Ideal for bulk procurement, resale, or in house IT use.
Third Party Quality Assurance Services
We provide independent quality audits to help businesses and refurbishers meet industry standards before resale or distribution.
Certified Data Wipe and Compliance
All devices undergo secure data erasure using NIST 800 88 protocols. This ensures complete data protection and compliance with global regulations.
Device Buyback and Trade In Programs
Turn idle or aging hardware into value through structured trade in and buyback services. Reduce electronic waste and increase return on investment.
Custom Configuration and Software Imaging
We offer tailored device setup with custom OS imaging, software installation, and hardware upgrades based on your enterprise needs.
Bulk Packaging and White Label Options
Opt for sustainable packaging or white label shipments that align with your brand. A seamless option for resellers and partners.
Warranty and Ongoing Support Plans
Select from extended warranty options and reliable remote or on site support packages designed specifically for enterprise clients.

Driving Sustainable Innovation Through Tech

In partnership with Azerbaijan Technical University, Dithari Innovation & Sustainability Center (DISC), empowers future-ready talent in IT sustainability, circular economy, e-waste management, and ESG practices. Through hackathons, hands-on training, certifications, and real-world projects, DISC blends academic learning with practical experience. With advanced labs and expert mentorship, it drives green tech innovation and prepares students for impactful careers in sustainable technology.

Products & Inventory
What types of devices does Dithari offer?

We supply certified refurbished laptops, smartphones, tablets, and accessories from trusted global brands.

Are the refurbished devices original?

Yes, all devices are 100% original and verified through strict sourcing and testing protocols.

Do the products come with accessories?

Yes, devices include essential accessories like certified chargers and cables. Bulk and eco-friendly packaging options are available.

How are devices graded?

We use a standard grading system (A, B, C) based on cosmetic condition—while all devices meet high functional performance standards.
