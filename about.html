Who We Are

Dithari is a UAE-based B2B supplier of certified refurbished tech products, including laptops, smartphones, and IT equipment from top brands. We help businesses save costs, reduce e-waste, and access high-quality tech at unbeatable prices. Built for resellers, companies, and institutions, Dithari powers smart, sustainable, and affordable technology solutions.
